Files are processed in the browser, not uploaded to GotoCalm. Derived metadata, candidate locators and limited source excerpts (up to 180 characters per retained section) may remain in the tab draft and user-controlled exports. Do not select documents containing personal data, confidential or client information, credentials, vessel details, security-sensitive material, or live operational information. Automated cues may be incomplete or wrong. You must confirm the assessment object, consequence class, jurisdiction, market role and every candidate locator.

The doctrine-hosted Screening may offer a separate, explicit opt-in to contribute a minimal summary to its public activity aggregate. That contribution does not include selected files, raw text, individual answers, scores, free text or evidence locators. Declining does not restrict local screening.
