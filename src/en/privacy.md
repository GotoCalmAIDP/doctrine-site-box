## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit this website.

---

## Data Collection and Tracking

The site operators do not run analytics, tracking scripts, or user profiling mechanisms on this website.

The site operators do not request names, accounts or personal profile information through the screening. Local and optional aggregate processing are described below.

---

## Local Screening and Document Processing

Screening answers, evidence-workspace fields and document analysis remain in the current browser tab unless you choose to export a JSON snapshot. The site does not automatically save them.

The DOCX intake runs only after you select a file. Processing occurs locally in the browser. The file and its raw text are not uploaded or retained by the site operators. After analysis, the interface keeps only derived metadata and candidate section locators in the tab. Do not select files containing personal, confidential, client, security-sensitive or live operational information.

## Optional Public Aggregate

After a substantive screening, you may separately opt in to send a minimal summary consisting of the public triage class, context lens, screening outcome, declared answer basis and a random duplicate-control token. It excludes the selected file, individual answers, percentages, free text and evidence locators.

The summary is used only for a rolling, thresholded public activity aggregate. The public distribution is withheld until at least 10 consented substantive completions are present. The site operators do not request a name or account for this contribution. Infrastructure providers may nonetheless create ordinary technical logs as described below.

## Infrastructure and Hosting

This site is hosted on **GitHub Pages**.

Infrastructure-level logs or cookies may exist through the hosting provider as part of normal operations, but these are not controlled or accessed by the site operators.

Please refer to [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement) for details regarding their infrastructure.
