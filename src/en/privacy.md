## Introduction

## Data Collection and Tracking

The site does not deploy advertising trackers or cross-site profiling. It does not require an account or personal profile to use Screening. The optional public activity aggregate is described separately below; it is not a record of the visitor's documents or answers.

---

## Local Screening and Document Processing

Screening answers, evidence-workspace fields, derived document-analysis information, the local completion count and last result may be saved in this origin's `sessionStorage`. Refreshing, navigating within the tab or changing language can restore them. This tab draft is not sent to the site operator and is not automatically shared with gotocalm.com. If browser storage is unavailable, the tool displays a notice and local evaluation remains available without reliable draft persistence.

“Start another” clears the current assessment while retaining the tab's completion count, last result and Terms acceptance. Full Reset clears this tool's draft, count, last result and acceptance. Browser session restoration may restore tab data, so closing a tab is not a guaranteed deletion method. Reset does not erase downloaded files, copies you made elsewhere, or a summary already submitted to the aggregate service.

The accepted Terms version and acceptance timestamp are stored only in this tab draft. They are not transmitted, are not an operator-held consent log and do not opt you into the public aggregate. Framework rights metadata in exported JSON contains no acceptance timestamp.

Document intake starts only after explicit file selection and checks type and size before reading. It accepts up to three DOCX, UTF-8 TXT or Markdown files, each no larger than 10 MiB (10,485,760 bytes), selected one at a time. DOCX must not be macro-enabled or password-protected; additional extraction limits apply. Files and full raw text are processed locally and are not uploaded by the tool. Derived metadata, candidate section locators and limited source excerpts (up to 180 characters per retained section) may remain in the tab draft. Do not select files containing personal, confidential, client, vessel, security-sensitive or live operational information.

Exports are created locally when you choose to export. They can contain your entered context, answers, evidence fields and derived document information. Inspect an exported file before sharing it; labels, locators and excerpts may themselves reveal information. Imports preserve the supplied basis and do not verify the underlying evidence. GotoCalm does not receive an export merely because you create or download it.

## Optional Public Aggregate

The doctrine Screening can request a public aggregate display from the separate GotoCalm aggregate service at `goto-calm-screening-data.partasyukvadym.chatgpt.site`. Loading that display does not submit your screening result. A network request nevertheless exposes ordinary connection information to the receiving infrastructure, as described below.

After a substantive screening, you may separately opt in and choose to submit a minimal summary: consequence class, context lens, screening outcome, declared answer basis, screening version and a random duplicate-control token. It excludes selected files, individual answers, scores, free text and evidence locators. Accepting Terms does not select this contribution; declining it does not restrict local screening. The commercial Screening on gotocalm.com has both aggregate display requests and contributions disabled.

The summary supports a thresholded public activity display. Its rolling 30-day display window and threshold of at least 10 consented substantive completions describe what is shown, not a promise that all service records or infrastructure logs are deleted after 30 days. No name or account is requested for the contribution. Contact the operator with questions about a contribution or service-held information; a local Reset does not withdraw a summary already sent.

## Infrastructure and Hosting

The doctrine site's static pages and assets are hosted on **GitHub Pages**. The optional aggregate is provided by the separate service identified above. Hosting and service providers may process ordinary request information, such as IP address, browser details and access time, for delivery, security and operation under their policies. This notice does not promise that no infrastructure logs exist or that the operator can never access service logs.

See [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement) for the static host's practices. Following an external link leaves this site. Email you choose to send to the contact address is a separate communication handled by your email provider and the recipient's provider; it is not part of local browser processing. Do not send confidential or sensitive materials without an agreed information boundary and channel.
